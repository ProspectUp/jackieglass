A few things you might be wondering

Before you begin.

Is the AI concept really complimentary?+

That’s the proposed offer: one complimentary concept to help you explore a fresh direction, with no obligation to book a consultation. This interactive mockup demonstrates the experience with an example image.

Is this a finished design for my home?+

An AI concept is a starting point for inspiration. It is not a measured design, renovation plan, quotation, or a promise that specific products or changes will work in your space. Jackie’s consultation explores what’s possible in your actual home.

What happens in the in-home consultation?+

Jackie’s initial consultation is a focused two-hour visit to discuss your goals, walk through your home, and provide recommendations and next steps. The consultation is $950. The calendar in this mockup shows sample availability and does not book an appointment.

Where does Jackie work?+

Jackie is based in Oakville and serves Burlington, Mississauga and the Greater Toronto Area. Your location and project can be discussed when arranging your consultation.
